### Algebraic and Transcendental Equations
- Finding the root of the equation using:
  - Bisection Method
  - Newton Raphson Method
  - Iteration Method
  - Method of False Position

## UNIT-II

### Finite Differences and Interpolation
- Finite Differences
  - Forward Differences
  - Backward Differences
- Newton’s:
  - Forward Difference Interpolation Formula
  - Backward Difference Interpolation Formula
- Lagrange’s Interpolating Polynomial

> *(Problems only)*

---

## UNIT-III

### Statistics
- Measures of Central Tendency
- Measures of Dispersion
- Moments
- Measures of:
  - Skewness
  - Kurtosis

---

## UNIT-IV

### Probability
- Theory of Probability
- Definitions of Probability
- Sample Space
- Probability of an Event
- Independence of Events
- Theorems on Probability
- Conditional Probability
- Bayes' Theorem

---

## UNIT-V

### Correlation and Regression
- Correlation and Regression
- Properties of Correlation and Regression Coefficients
- Numerical Problems for Finding:
  - Correlation Coefficients
  - Regression Coefficients
